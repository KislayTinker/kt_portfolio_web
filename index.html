A tiny K-Means clustering algorithm, running live in your browser. The same idea behind my customer-segmentation work: find natural groups in unlabeled data.

K-Means, from scratch

Points scatter into hidden blobs. K-Means guesses k cluster centers, assigns each point to its nearest center, then moves the centers to the mean of their members — repeating until things settle.
